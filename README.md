# জনকণ্ঠ — Public Voice
Public Voice — Smart Citizen Complaint Management System

Public Voice is a proposed centralized digital platform for managing citizen complaints. It allows citizens to submit complaints online, track their progress, upload evidence, and provide feedback, while enabling government officers to manage, investigate, resolve and escalate complaints efficiently.

Key Features

- Citizen registration, login, and complaint submission
- Complaint tracking and status history
- Automatic complaint routing and officer assignment
- Evidence and investigation report management
- Configurable complaint escalation
- Notifications and citizen feedback
- Role-based access for citizens, officers, ministries, and administrators
- Reporting, analytics, and audit logging
